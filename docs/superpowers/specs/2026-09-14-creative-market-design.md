# Creative Market joint-launch design

User direction: launch Pamsika Creative Market alongside AdLab. Pending optional business-model clarification, implement the curated services model. Businesses browse disciplines and submit a matching brief; creatives apply with their portfolio. This extends the existing private website and durable enquiry system. It does not claim a staffed roster or promise a match.

Use the approved parent wordmark with adjacent live text outside its clear-space frame. Preserve all immutable logo assets, Sora/Inter, approved palette and tokens. Light surfaces, solid listing cards and accessible category/search controls follow the marketplace kit. Its example people, prices, reviews and availability are specimen data and must not ship.

Routes: /creative-market with searchable six-discipline catalogue; /creative-market/[slug] for scope guidance; /creative-market/request for a brief; /creative-market/join for a creator application. Disciplines: film and video, design and branding, photography, writing and content, sound and voice, animation and motion. Each detail page explains possible deliverables, briefing inputs and how it connects to AdLab. No invented prices, turnaround promises or service bookings.

Extend existing enquiry kinds with market and creator. Market needs business name and at least one supported discipline; creator needs discipline, location and a valid HTTPS portfolio URL, with business/studio optional. Reuse review/edit, server validation, consent, idempotent D1 storage, failures and receipt. Store portfolio within the existing details JSON; no schema migration. A submitted application is pending review, not an approved profile. No emails are sent. Request submission is not a booking.

Home, navigation, footer, services, about and metadata introduce both launch offerings, with AI Business Studio remaining future. AdLab stays the flagship full-campaign offering. Creative Market supports specific creative needs. Public release still needs an operational owner for enquiries and real approved creator supply.
